# Product specialization — Watches and horology

## Scope

Brand, collection, commercial reference, generation, variant and market. Distinguish movement/calibre, model, case reference, dial variant and edition.

## Specialized sections

- Collection origin, design, creators, historical context, presentation/production years.
- Exact movement, origin, winding, frequency (vibrations/hour or Hz), reserve, relevant jewel count, complications and advertised/certified timekeeping.
- Diameter, thickness, materials, back/crystal, advertised water resistance and conditions, crown, bracelet and dials.
- References, periods, movement/material differences; limited editions/quantities only where documented.
- Manufacturer catalogues, brand archives, patents, horological books and attributed auction catalogues.
- Servicing, parts availability, documented reliability and regional after-sales policies.
- Separate period list price, auction estimate, hammer price and listings; currency, taxes and specimen condition.
- Describe verifiable authenticity/serial criteria without authenticating a specific watch from insufficient evidence.

## Pitfalls

Unsupported designer attribution, estimates repeated as certified production, asking prices treated as transactions, movement/reference confusion, historical water resistance presented as a current guarantee.
