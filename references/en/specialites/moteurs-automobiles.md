# Product specialization — Automotive engines

## Required identity

Exact engine code (e.g. N54B30), manufacturer, family, displacement, market-specific years, architecture and power variants. Separate engine, vehicle model and part reference.

## Technical matrix

- Cylinder count/layout, bore/stroke, displacement, compression ratio, valve train and timing.
- Direct/indirect injection, induction, turbo/compressor count/type, lubrication and cooling.
- Power/torque by variant and measurement standard, engine speed, market, year and calibration.
- Emissions, prescribed fuel and version-specific standards.
- Equipped vehicle models with chassis codes, years, markets and confirmed exceptions.
- Hardware evolution, superseded part references, manufacturer publications and compatibility.

## Reliability and maintenance

For each defect record symptoms, affected parts, documented cause, severity, periods/versions, manufacturer campaign, service bulletin, revised references, remedy and sourced costs. Never infer failure rates from forum anecdotes. Label suspected causes, localize costs and leave diagnosis to competent technicians.

## Sources and controls

Manufacturer/workshop manuals, service bulletins, NHTSA/KBA/DVSA and relevant local recall authorities, Bosch/supplier documentation where relevant, patents, documented dynamometer tests and independent reviews. Observed issues are not automatically regulatory recalls.

## Research route

BMW N54 → establish engine family and variants → equipped-model matrix → specifications by standard/year → sourced defect inventory → country-specific bulletin/recall review → fixes and prevalence limitations → confidence/coverage audit.
