# Product specialization — Computing, software and platforms

## Research

- Publisher/manufacturer, rights holders, version, architecture, support, distribution and year.
- Target system, dependencies, CPU/OS/API platform, protocols, licences and compatibility.
- Innovations and prior art: invention, adoption and popularization differ; individual and collective attribution.
- Version matrix: release, end of support, compatibility, breaking changes, security and availability.
- Performance/security: supplier claims versus independent measurements, CVEs, fixes and field reports.
- Deployment/maintenance: official documentation, APIs, logical architecture, data models, migration and obsolescence.
- Product history: commercial pivots, acquisitions, forks, integrations and successors.

## Examples and sources

Separate NeXT Computer/NeXTcube and NEXTSTEP/OPENSTEP/Mac OS X while establishing ancestry. NeXT machines used in developing the Web do not make NeXT its inventor. Use vendor guides, RFCs/standards, release notes, public code, historical repositories, official vulnerability registers, original announcements, museums/computing archives and independent technical studies.
