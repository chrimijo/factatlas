## Product specializations

Engine codes, displacement, automotive engine families: `specialites/moteurs-automobiles.md`. Watch references and movements: `specialites/montres.md`. Software, platforms, electronics and IT: `specialites/technologies-numeriques.md`. For other products use `modules/produits.md` with sector-specific sections.

## Scope checks

BMW may mean a manufacturer, legal group, vehicle brand or product range; BMW N54 is an engine. Rolex company, brand and Submariner reference are distinct, and watch series are not movement generations. NeXT includes a company, brand, hardware workstations, NEXTSTEP, OPENSTEP and WebObjects. Genève may mean city, canton or urban area: keep statistics separate. Suisse/Switzerland requires national versus cantonal distinctions. Château de Chillon is a specific monument in Veytaux; lake, municipality and castle have distinct boundaries.

Separate legal, commercial, geographic, technical and historical identity. Date name changes, parent/subsidiary relationships and product succession without merging distinct entities. Apply [language policy](langues.md) to every field; retain original names and authentic quotations.
