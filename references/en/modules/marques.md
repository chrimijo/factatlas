# Module — Brands

A brand is not necessarily a legal entity. Establish rights holder, commercial operator, owner group, territory and period.

## Sections

- Trade name, documented purpose/meaning, country of origin; distinguish registration, creation and first-use dates.
- Creator, applicant, current owner, licensing, ownership changes, mergers and distribution scope.
- Dated logo/visual identity/slogan history and proven design attribution.
- Product/service portfolio, notable families/series, life cycles and innovations actually attributable to the brand.
- Target segments, markets, channels, international presence and documented strategies without promotional adjectives.
- Intellectual property, trademark litigation, repositioning, controversies and verified recalls.
- Prices, awareness or brand-value rankings only with organization, year, metric and method.

## Sources and pitfalls

WIPO/EUIPO/USPTO/national trademark offices, registration files, dated releases, period catalogues, archived advertising (evidence of presentation, not performance), design museums and documented histories. Do not confuse brand and company, registration and launch, unproven design/invention attribution, or marketing rankings and accounting value.
