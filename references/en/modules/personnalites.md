# Module — People and biographies

## Identity and scope

Full/public name and aliases, research-relevant identity, relevant nationalities, verified biographical dates/places, principal role and associated organizations/movements. Never invent private details or include unnecessary sensitive information.

## Research questions

1. Origins and education: dates, institutions, qualifications and accurate description of unfinished studies.
2. Career timeline: positions, responsibilities, employers/organizations, start/end years and nature of involvement.
3. Achievements: works, publications, inventions, films, albums, offices and awards. Separate personal, collaborative and organizational achievements.
4. Works inventory: first publication/release year, original title, exact role (author, director, producer, inventor), publisher/producer/place and a factual one-sentence description. Distinguish reissues, translations and expanded editions.
5. Influence: documented collaboration and recognized consequences; attribute value judgments.
6. Recognition/controversies: official distinctions, attested historical debates and explicit legal procedural status.
7. Authenticated quotations: five maximum by default, verified wording, context, date and primary source where possible. Keep original wording and label any translation separately.

## Specialist sources

Institutional/public civil registers where relevant; national library catalogues (BnF, Library of Congress, VIAF), publishers, ISBN, patents, universities, audiovisual archives, award bodies, documented biographies and original interview transcripts. Official biographies are not independent validation.

## Risks

Do not attribute an entire company's work to its founder, treat rumors or promotional self-portraits as proof, claim a complete works inventory without a method, or present unresolved allegations as established facts.

## Language and multilingual research — v1.1.0

Follow `../langues.md`: user-language detection with priority `--lang fr|en`. Localize every field, table, source status and limitation. Search French, English and relevant original-document languages, and check originals. Retain proper names, original titles, codes, references and authentic quotes; label translations separately. Domain requirements are identical in French and English.
