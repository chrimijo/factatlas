# Module — Countries, states and territories

## Classification

Official/common names, disputed recognition or territorial status, capitals by function, government form and administrative organization. Distinguish state, nation, geographic country and dependent territory.

## Content

1. Geography, borders, area, climates, resources, environmental protection and hazards.
2. Dated population, census/projection method, urbanization, languages and social indicators.
3. History, independence/constitution, regime and border changes.
4. Institutions: constitution, powers, electoral system, administration, courts and dated elections/leaders.
5. Economy: distinguish nominal/PPP GDP, growth, inflation, unemployment definitions, budget, debt, trade, industries and income; specify year, currency and sources.
6. Health, education, housing, energy, transport, digital access and inequality with robust evidence.
7. Defence, alliances, international relations/institutions and legal commitments.
8. Culture, heritage, languages, literature, science and sport in context.
9. Current issues, risks and controversies: attributed views and care with contested subjects.

## Sources and pitfalls

National statistical offices, laws/constitutions, IMF, World Bank, UN/agencies, OECD/Eurostat as relevant, central banks, public administrations, independent scholarship and historical archives. Distinguish calendar/fiscal years, current/constant GDP, PPP/nominal, residents/citizens, national/regional indicators. Avoid one-sided treatment of contested policy and old statistics presented as current.
