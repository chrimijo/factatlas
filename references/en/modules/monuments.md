# Module — Monuments, buildings and heritage sites

## Identification

Name, linguistic/historical variants, function, exact location, owner/manager and protection status. Separate building, architectural ensemble, archaeological site and associated intangible heritage.

## Documentary questions

- Construction dates by phase and evidence type: archives, material dating, archaeology.
- Patrons, designers, architects, project managers and craftspeople; certain attribution versus hypothesis.
- Style, plan, dimensions, materials, construction techniques, alterations and extensions.
- Successive uses, historical events, occupants and ownership changes.
- Listings/inventories: authority, date, category, scope and limitations.
- Restoration, damage, conservation campaigns, modern techniques and documented current condition.
- Excavations, discoveries, interpretations, scholarly disputes and degree of agreement.
- Visitor access/management only when requested or useful; hours/prices need recent verification.

## Sources and pitfalls

Heritage authorities/UNESCO where relevant, national inventories, architects' archives, archaeological studies, historical monographs, restoration records, museums and authenticated dated photos. Do not present local legends as fact, founding dates as the date of the current structure, enclosure dimensions as monument dimensions, or UNESCO tentative listing as World Heritage inscription.
