# Module — Companies and organizations

## Legal identity

Legal name and successive forms, useful register/identifier, incorporation/closure, dated headquarters, country, founders, leaders, parents/subsidiaries, owned brands, activity and private/listed/public status. Distinguish holding company, consolidated group, operating entity, brand and establishment.

## Sector research

1. Origins, founders, milestones and name changes.
2. Dated established evidence for ownership, control, governance and organization charts.
3. Activities, divisions, locations, value chain, markets and competitors using comparable criteria.
4. Product/service catalogue: families, generations, years and references where published; inventions, patents, licences and collective inventions.
5. Capital and transactions: funding, acquisitions, mergers, disposals, IPO, liquidation/restructuring; announcement versus completion.
6. Five available fiscal years by default: year, currency, standards, scope and audited/statement/estimated provenance; revenue, profit, headcount, debt and other established indicators with cautious comparisons.
7. Regulation, litigation, controversies and risks with procedural status.
8. Current situation and last checked document date.

## Typical discrepancies

Acquisition accounting cost, cash outflow, securities value and assumed debt differ. Consolidated group figures, subsidiary figures and media estimates differ. Annual revenue, nine-month revenue, orders and valuation differ. An abandoned activity is not a dissolved company; a discontinued brand is not a deregistered entity. Apply `../contradictions-chiffrees.md` whenever figures disagree.

## Sources

Annual reports, 10-K/20-F filings, official company registers, original transaction releases, public minutes, regulators, company archives, central banks/statistical offices, independent press and investigations for cross-checking.
