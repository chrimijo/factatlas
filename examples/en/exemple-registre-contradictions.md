# Teaching example — discrepancy register

**Fictional figures for illustration only; never attribute them to a real company.**

Question: what amount did an acquirer pay for a company?

| Source | Figure | Definition | Evidence status |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Accounting report | $420 million | Total acquisition cost including assumed liabilities | Fictional primary source |
| Financial release | $350 million | Direct payments to shareholders | Fictional primary source |
| Press report | $425 million | Announced amount without breakdown | Fictional secondary source |

Select $420 million only for total recorded cost, which includes items absent from shareholder payments; use $350 million for those payments. Keep $425 million unreconciled until its scope is established. Status is `perimetre` (scope difference) for 420/350; `provisoire` (provisional) or `non_resolue` (unresolved) for 425, depending on evidence. Do not average them. Apply this method to actually verified figures, never import these fictional ones into real research. French counterpart: [example](../exemple-registre-contradictions.md).
